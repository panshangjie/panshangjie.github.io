# Shangjie Pan

Shangjie Pan is currently a Specially Appointed Associate Professor with the Innovation School of Integrated Circuits, Hefei University of Technology.

He received his Ph.D. degree from the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences. His research interests include Trusted Execution Environments, RISC-V Security, and Hardware Security.
His research has been published in international conferences and journals, including DAC, ICCD, ASP-DAC, and IEEE TCAD.
